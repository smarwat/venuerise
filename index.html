FAQ

Questions we always get asked

How quickly will I see results? +
Most clients see a meaningful increase in inquiries within the first 30 days. Significant booking lifts typically happen between days 30–90 as we optimize and refine targeting based on real data from your market.
What's the minimum ad budget required? +
We recommend a minimum ad spend of $1,500/month for meaningful results. Most clients spend $2,000–$5,000/month. Our management fee is separate and tiered based on scope.
Do you work with venues outside the US? +
Yes. We work with venues in the US, Canada, UK, and Australia. Strategies adapt to each local market including platform preferences, seasonality, and audience behavior.
What types of venues do you work with? +
Wedding venues, banquet halls, event spaces, rooftop venues, garden venues, ballrooms, and multi-purpose facilities. If you rent your space for events, we can scale your bookings.
Do I need to sign a long-term contract? +
No long-term contracts. Month-to-month with 30-day cancellation notice. Our results speak for themselves — the average VenueRise client stays 18+ months.
What does onboarding look like? +
Onboarding takes 7–10 business days. We handle everything — ad account setup, pixel installation, content creation, and campaign builds. We'll need platform access and some venue visuals.
