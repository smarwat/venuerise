# Post-Demo Follow-Up

**Rule:** send the recap email within **2 hours** of the demo ending. Faster = higher close rate.

Every email below is a template — fill in the specifics from the demo itself. The recap email's job isn't to summarize the product; it's to summarize **what they told you** about their pain.

---

## Email 1 — Follow-up after a good demo

**Subject:** `VenueRise follow-up — Revenue Recovery pilot for [Venue Name]`

**Body:**

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for the 10 minutes today. Quick recap:

What you mentioned hurts most:
  - [pain 1 in their words]
  - [pain 2 in their words]
  - [pain 3 in their words]

What VenueRise showed:
  - [leak surface from demo, e.g. "Slow first reply queue with ~5 leads
    sitting > 1h"]
  - [leak surface, e.g. "$XYZ pipeline at risk in the leakage dashboard"]
  - [leak surface, e.g. "Source attribution showing which inquiry
    sources are becoming bookings"]

Next step:

A 30-Day Revenue Recovery Pilot installed with your team. We
configure venue profile, lead sources, brand voice, knowledge base,
and tour availability — then walk the leaks with you weekly so the
team can recover more inquiries into tours.

Pilot fee for a venue at [Venue Name]'s scale lands in the [low/mid]
four-figure range per month. I'll send a written proposal with the
exact scope this week if that helps.

Let me know if there's a stakeholder I should loop in, or if you'd
rather review the proposal first and then decide.

[Your name]
```

**Why this works:**
- Leads with *their* language (the pain quotes), not yours
- Names the specific surfaces they saw, so it doesn't feel generic
- Gives the next step clearly without being pushy
- Acknowledges the buying process (stakeholders, proposal-first)

## Email 2 — Follow-up after a demo no-show

**Subject:** `Missed you for the demo — reschedule?`

**Body:**

```
Hi [Name],

Looks like the demo didn't connect today. Totally fine — things come
up.

If you still want to see how VenueRise catches revenue leaks across
your inquiry sources, here are two slots that work this week:

  - [day, date, time]
  - [day, date, time]

Or pick whatever works on my calendar: [link]

If timing's just bad right now, no worries — happy to circle back in
a few weeks.

[Your name]
```

**Don't:** chase a no-show more than twice. The third "let's reschedule" looks desperate. If they no-show twice, drop them to a 30-day check-in queue.

## Email 3 — Follow-up after "Send me info"

**Subject:** `Info you asked for — VenueRise for [Venue Name]`

**Body:**

```
Hi [Name],

As promised — the short version of what we do:

VenueRise is an AI Revenue OS for wedding venues. It connects your
website, Instagram, The Knot, WeddingWire, Meta Ads, and inbox
inquiries into one dashboard, shows which leads are slipping, and
drafts the next reply for your team to approve.

The honest version: it's most useful for venues with 30+ inquiries
a month across multiple sources who feel like good leads are
slipping faster than the team can follow up.

If that sounds like [Venue Name], here are two ways to go deeper:

  1. 10-min walkthrough on a realistic venue pipeline — [calendar link]
  2. One-pager with the pilot details — [link or attachment]

Either way, no pressure.

[Your name]
```

**Tip:** "Send me info" is sometimes a polite no. The conversion rate from info-send → demo is 10–20%. Don't put all your energy here — keep the email tight, link the one-pager, and move on.

## Email 4 — Follow-up after an objection

Use this when the demo went well but they raised a specific blocker (e.g. "We need to talk to our coordinator first," "We need to think about pricing," "We need to wait until after the busy season").

**Subject:** `Quick thought on [their concern]`

**Body:**

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks again for the walkthrough yesterday.

You mentioned [their specific concern]. Wanted to send a quick note
on that:

[Specific response to the concern — see docs/gtm/OBJECTION-HANDLING.md
for templates. Customize to their venue.]

If that addresses it, happy to set up a follow-up with [the
stakeholder they need to loop in / a smaller-scope option / a later
timing window].

If not, totally understand — let me know what would actually help and
I'll work from there.

[Your name]
```

## Email 5 — Follow-up after pricing conversation

If they asked for pricing and you quoted a range on the call, follow up with the written version:

**Subject:** `VenueRise pilot proposal — [Venue Name]`

**Body:**

```
Hi [Name],

Pilot proposal for [Venue Name] below. Held to one page so it's
readable without scrolling.

────────────────────────────────────────

30-Day Revenue Recovery Pilot — VenueRise

Tier: [Starter / Growth / Premium]
Setup fee: $X
Monthly pilot fee: $X
Start date: [yyyy-mm-dd]

What we configure (week 1):
  - Venue profile
  - Lead-source mapping ([list the specific sources they mentioned])
  - Knowledge base (FAQs, pricing guidance, policies)
  - Brand voice (tone, sample replies, phrases)
  - Tour availability + blackouts
  - Operator training

What runs daily (weeks 1–4):
  - Inquiry intake from all configured channels
  - AI drafts per lead, gated by operator approval
  - Revenue leakage dashboard live
  - Recovery / reactivation / tour-confirmation queues
  - Source attribution tracking

What we review weekly (4 sessions):
  - Walk the dashboard with the team
  - Surface highest-value next actions
  - Tune brand voice + thresholds based on real data
  - Track outcome metrics

What we measure (end-of-pilot review):
  - Response time improvement
  - Stale leads recovered
  - Qualified leads moved to tour
  - Tours confirmed
  - Booked opportunities the system influenced
  - Source-to-booked visibility

What we don't promise:
  - Specific booking increase (no guaranteed revenue)
  - Autonomous sending (every reply is operator-approved)
  - SOC 2 / GDPR certification (questionnaire available on request)

Cancellation: 7-day notice, refund of unused portion of monthly fee.

────────────────────────────────────────

Happy to walk through any of this on a 15-min follow-up. Or if you're
ready to start, I can send a DocuSign and we kick off [proposed date].

[Your name]
```

**Send within 24 hours** of the demo call. The longer you wait, the colder the deal gets.

## Email 6 — Long-term nurture (90-day check-in)

For prospects who said "not now" or went dark after multiple touches:

**Subject:** `Quick check-in on [Venue Name]'s inquiry workflow`

**Body:**

```
Hi [Name],

Circling back after a few months.

When we last talked, [their situation — e.g. "you were heading into
peak season and not ready to add anything new" / "the coordinator
seat was in transition"]. Wanted to check if the timing's any
different now.

If revenue recovery from inquiries is on the table, happy to show
you what we've built since — [one specific thing that changed, e.g.
"we now support Instagram DMs end-to-end" or "we added a weekly
operator digest"].

If it's still not the right moment, totally fine — I'll set another
check-in for a few months out.

[Your name]
```

**Cadence:** 90 days between nurture touches. After 3 nurture touches with no engagement, archive the lead.

```
  - Weekly reviews (recurring same slot)
  - End-of-pilot review: [proposed date/time]

  2. 2–3 examples of replies your team has sent that you'd consider

  4. FAQ / policy doc if you have one (informal is fine)

If you want to send those as a reply to this email, I'll have the
setup call ready to roll.

Looking forward to it.

[Your name]
```

## Tracking follow-up outcomes

After every follow-up email, log:

```
Email sent: [type — recap / no-show / info / objection / pricing / nurture]
Subject:
Date sent:
Reply: yes / no / partial
If yes: what did they say (one quote)?
Next step:
Next step date:
```

The most useful pattern this surfaces: which email types actually convert. Most teams find that the **recap email** and the **breakup-style nurture** convert at 5–10× the rate of the "send me info" follow-up.
