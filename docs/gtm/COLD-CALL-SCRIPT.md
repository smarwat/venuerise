# Cold Call Script

**Goal of a cold call:** book a 10-minute demo. Nothing else.

Don't try to close on the call. Don't try to qualify too deeply. Don't try to pitch features. The job is to earn 10 minutes on the calendar.

---

## The 30-second opener

```
Hi, is this [Name]? I'll be quick.

I'm working with wedding venues on a simple problem: a lot of venues
are not losing weddings because they lack leads — they're losing them
because inquiries from the website, Instagram, The Knot, WeddingWire,
and ads don't always get followed up fast enough or pushed to a tour.

We built VenueRise to show which leads are slipping, what they're
worth, and draft the next follow-up for the team to approve.

Are you the right person to ask about how your venue handles wedding
inquiries?
```

**Why this works:**
- "I'll be quick" gives them permission to listen for 30 seconds
- The problem statement is venue-specific, not SaaS-generic
- The close is a question they can say yes to without committing to anything

---

## Branching responses

### If they say "What is it?"

```
It's an AI Revenue OS for wedding venues. It connects your inquiry
sources into one dashboard, flags revenue leaks like slow replies or
qualified leads with no tour, and helps your team follow up before
good leads go cold.
```

Keep it to 3 sentences. If they want more, they'll ask.

---

### If they say "We already use [HoneyBook / Tripleseat / a CRM]"

**Pattern interrupt:**

```
That's fine — I'm not calling to replace your CRM or calendar. This
sits above your current process and shows where revenue is leaking
from follow-up gaps. Your CRM stores the lead. VenueRise tells your
team which leads are slipping right now and what to do about it.
```

---

### If they say "Send me information"

```
Happy to. But honestly the page won't tell you whether this matters
for your venue specifically — the value is in seeing your own
fragmented inquiries in one place. Would 10 minutes next week work
just to see if there's a fit?
```

If they still want info: send the one-pager (`docs/gtm/ONE-PAGE-PITCH.md` exported, or the homepage URL). Schedule a 3-day follow-up.

---

### If they say "We get too many leads already"

```
That's actually why this matters. The goal isn't more leads — it's
converting more of the leads you already paid for into tours and
bookings. If you're at capacity it usually means the higher-fit leads
are getting lost in the volume, not that there's no problem to solve.
```

---

### If they say "We're not interested"

Don't push. Get permission to follow up.

```
Totally fair. Can I check back in a couple of months in case the
follow-up situation changes? Otherwise I'll leave you alone.
```

---

## Discovery questions (only if they engage)

Ask one or two — not all of them. The point is to surface a pain that justifies the demo.

- "How many wedding inquiries does the venue typically get per month?"
- "Where do most of those leads come from — website, The Knot, Instagram?"
- "Who replies to inquiries when they come in?"
- "How quickly do you usually respond?"
- "What happens when an inquiry goes quiet for a week or two?"
- "How many tours are you booking each month?"
- "Do you have a clear view of which sources actually produce booked weddings?"
- "Do you ever feel like leads slip through the cracks?"

If you hear "I don't know" or "we don't really track that" twice in a row — that's the moment to ask for the demo.

---

## Soft close

```
If I could show you a 10-minute walkthrough of how this catches slow
replies, stale leads, and unbooked tour opportunities, would that be
worth seeing?
```

Booking the demo is the win. If they say yes, send the calendar link right then on the call. Don't say "I'll email you a link" — that's where momentum dies.

---

## Harder close (for skeptical or busy owners)

```
If one additional wedding booking could cover the cost of the pilot
many times over, would you be open to testing this for 30 days?
```

This shifts the conversation from "is this a feature I need" to "is one more wedding worth $X to me." For most venues, the answer is obviously yes — which makes the demo the natural next step.

## Voicemail script (under 25 seconds)

```
Hi [Name], this is [your name] from VenueRise. I work with wedding
venues on a specific problem — strong inquiries going cold from slow
follow-up.

If you want to know if it applies to your venue, I'm at [phone] or
[email]. No pressure either way. Thanks.
```

**Don't ramble.** Don't pitch the product on voicemail. Leave a number and a clean reason to call back.

---

## Gatekeeper script (front desk / coordinator)

```
Hi, this is [your name]. I'm calling about how the venue handles
wedding inquiries from sources like The Knot, WeddingWire, and
Instagram. Is the owner or sales director the right person to talk
to, or is there a sales manager I should reach?
```

**Don't say:** "I'm calling to pitch a software product." The gatekeeper will block you.

**Do say:** something operational the gatekeeper can route. "Wedding inquiries" routes to sales/coordinator. "Marketing" routes to whoever handles ads.

If asked "Can I ask what this is about?":

```
It's about the workflow they use to follow up on couple inquiries.
Should take five minutes if they're around — happy to schedule
something otherwise.
```

## Call tracking

After every call, log in your CRM / spreadsheet:

- Date + time
- Contact name + role
- Outcome: `booked_demo` / `follow_up_later` / `not_interested` / `wrong_person` / `voicemail` / `gatekeeper_blocked`
- Notable quote (their words, not yours)
- Next step + date

The notable quote is the most valuable line. After 20 calls you'll see the same 3–4 pains come up over and over — that's your real pitch deck.

---

## What "good" looks like

Per 50 dials at the right ICP:

| Outcome | Realistic rate |
|---|---|
| Connect with a real person | 8–15 |
| Of those, get past the first 30 seconds | 4–8 |
| Of those, agree to a demo | 1–3 |
| Of demos, become a pilot | 1 in 4–6 |

Translation: **~50 dials = 1 paid pilot** at the early stage. The numbers improve as your script and ICP tighten.

If you're getting less than 1 connect per 10 dials, your list is wrong (not your script).
If you're connecting but never getting past the opener, your opener is wrong.
If you're getting past the opener but never booking demos, your ask is too soft.
