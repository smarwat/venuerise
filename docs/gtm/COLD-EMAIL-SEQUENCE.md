# Cold Email Sequence (5 emails)

**Goal:** book a 10-minute demo.

**Cadence:** Day 1 / Day 3 / Day 7 / Day 12 / Day 18.

**Rules:**
- Short. Mobile-readable. Under 100 words per email.
- Venue-specific framing, not SaaS-generic.
- No fake personalization. ("Loved your venue!" gets filtered as spam.)
- One CTA per email.
- No guaranteed revenue. No compliance claims.
- The breakup email is the highest-reply email in the sequence. Don't skip it.

---

## Email 1 — Day 1: pain / problem

**Subject lines (A/B test these):**
- `Are wedding inquiries slipping through the cracks?`
- `Quick question about [Venue Name]'s inquiry workflow`
- `Following up on leads from The Knot / Instagram / your site`

**Body:**

```
Hi [Name],

I'm reaching out because many wedding venues aren't short on leads —
they're short on visibility into which inquiries are slipping.

Between website forms, Instagram, The Knot, WeddingWire, Meta leads,
and email, strong couples can go cold simply because nobody replied
fast enough, pushed them to a tour, or followed up after they
ghosted.

We built VenueRise as an AI Revenue OS for wedding venues. It shows
which leads need action, what they may be worth, and drafts the next
reply for your team to approve.

Worth a 10-minute walkthrough?

[Your name]
```

## Email 5 — Day 18: breakup email

**Subject:**
- `Closing the loop on [Venue Name]`
- `Should I stop reaching out?`

**Body:**

```
Hi [Name],

I've sent a few notes and don't want to be the person filling your
inbox. Should I close the loop on my end?

If the timing's just bad, happy to circle back in a few months.

If revenue recovery from inquiries isn't a priority right now, I'll
leave you alone — totally fair.

Either way, no hard feelings.

[Your name]
```

**Why the breakup email works:** people who've been ignoring you reply to this one because it gives them a guilt-free way to either re-engage or close the door. It's almost always the highest-reply email in the sequence.

## Recommended cadence

| Day | Email | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Email 1 | Pain framing — channels & follow-up gaps |
| 3 | Email 2 | Specific scenario — Tuesday inquiry, Wednesday lost |
| 7 | Email 3 | Tour conversion gap |
| 12 | Email 4 | Source attribution / where is spend wasted |
| 18 | Email 5 | Breakup |

After Day 18, drop them from active sequence. Add to a 90-day quarterly check-in.

---

## Subject line testing

After 50+ sends, look at your open rates. Cutoffs to retire a subject line:

- Open rate < 25%: kill it
- Reply rate < 2%: kill the whole email body, not just the subject
- Spam rate > 0.5%: kill the subject and check for spammy words

Words to avoid in subject lines (trigger spam filters or feel sales-y):
`free`, `guaranteed`, `revolutionary`, `disrupt`, `transform`, `unlock`, exclamation marks, ALL CAPS.

Words that work for this buyer:
`Quick question`, `One more thought`, `Closing the loop`, `re:`, the venue's actual name.

---

## Personalization that's worth doing

For every email, spend 90 seconds on the venue first:

- **Look at their website** — note the headline ("rustic estate", "luxury ballroom", "garden ceremonies"). Mention it briefly in the first sentence if natural.
- **Check their Instagram** — note recent post topics. If they're actively posting reels of tours, that's a sign they care about tours specifically.
- **Look at their The Knot listing** if they have one — confirms they're paying for at least one external lead source.

Don't fake personalization. Don't say "Loved your latest reel!" If you didn't actually look, the buyer can tell.

The personalization isn't a flattery hook. It's evidence that you bothered to look — which is itself a signal you're not blasting 10,000 venues.

---

## When to stop the sequence early

Stop sending if:
- They reply with anything (move to manual conversation)
- They book a demo via your calendar link
- They unsubscribe / ask you to stop
- Their email bounces (mark the address dead)
- You discover the venue is permanently closed

---

## What "good" looks like

Per 100 sends to the right ICP at the right contacts:

| Metric | Realistic |
|---|---|
| Delivery rate | > 95% |
| Open rate (across full sequence) | 35–50% |
| Reply rate | 4–10% |
| Demos booked | 1–4 |
| Demos → pilots | 1 in 4–6 |

Translation: **~100 sends ≈ 1 paid pilot** at the early stage.

If reply rate is < 2% across 200+ sends, the sequence is the wrong angle for this ICP. Revisit the pain framing in Email 1.
