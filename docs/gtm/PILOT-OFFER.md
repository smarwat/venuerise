# Pilot Offer

The first product VenueRise sells is a **30-Day Revenue Recovery Pilot** — services-assisted, paid, with measurable outcomes.

This is the offer for every demo conversation. Don't sell a "platform subscription" out of the gate. Sell the pilot. Convert pilots to retainers after proof.

---

## The pitch (use this verbatim or close to it)

> The pilot is designed to answer one question: are there enough recoverable revenue leaks in your current inquiry process to justify keeping VenueRise long-term?
>
> We install it with you. We configure your venue profile, lead sources, brand voice, knowledge base, tour availability, and the revenue leakage dashboard. Then we walk the leaks together every week for the first 30 days, so your team can recover more inquiries into tours.

---

## What's included (every pilot, regardless of tier)

### Setup (week 1)
- Venue profile setup
- Lead-source mapping (website, Instagram, The Knot, WeddingWire, Meta Ads, email)
- Knowledge base setup (FAQs, pricing guidance, policies, package summaries)
- Brand voice setup (tone, formality, sample replies, phrases to use/avoid)
- Tour availability setup (windows + blackouts)
- Operator training (one 60-min session)

### Daily workflow (weeks 1–4)
- Inquiry intake from all configured channels
- AI draft per new lead, gated by operator approval
- Revenue leakage dashboard live
- Recovery / reactivation / tour-confirmation queues populated
- Source attribution tracking active

### Weekly review (4 sessions)
- We walk the dashboard with the owner / sales director / coordinator
- Surface the highest-value next actions
- Tune brand voice, KB entries, and leakage thresholds based on real data
- Track outcome metrics

---

## Pilot deliverables

By end of week 4, the venue has:

- ✅ A live Revenue Recovery dashboard against their real inquiry flow
- ✅ Lead-source visibility with attribution per inquiry
- ✅ Trained AI draft workflow integrated into daily team rhythm
- ✅ Recovery + reactivation queues running automatically
- ✅ Tour confirmation queue catching unconfirmed tours
- ✅ Source-to-booked tracking active
- ✅ Documented baseline of current leakage metrics

---

## Success metrics (measured in week 4 review)

- Response time improvement (median minutes from inquiry → first reply)
- Number of stale leads recovered (warm leads that re-engaged after recovery follow-up)
- Qualified leads moved into tours (count + percentage)
- Tours confirmed (count + confirmation rate)
- Booked opportunities the system influenced (signed contracts that touched a Recovery / Reactivation / Tour-Confirmation surface)
- Source visibility improvement (operator can answer: "Which source produced our bookings last month?")

These are **measurable, not promised.** If the metrics aren't there, that's a clear data point that the venue isn't a fit.

## Pricing — INTERNAL GUIDANCE ONLY

> **Do NOT put exact pricing on the public site.** Pricing stays behind a sales conversation until the price-discovery work is done.

### Tier 1 — Starter Pilot

- **Range:** $1,000–$1,500 / month
- **Fit:** smaller venue, < 30 inquiries / month, 1–2 active lead sources, simple workflow
- **Setup intensity:** manual but fast (1 setup call)
- **Weekly review:** 30 min
- **Setup fee:** $0–$1,000 (waive for high-intent buyers)

### Tier 2 — Growth Pilot (default)

- **Range:** $2,000–$3,000 / month
- **Fit:** mid-size venue, 30–80 inquiries / month, 3+ lead sources (including at least one paid)
- **Setup intensity:** deeper — multiple lead source integrations, larger KB, multi-coordinator team
- **Weekly review:** 60 min
- **Setup fee:** $1,000–$2,500

### Tier 3 — Premium Revenue Ops Pilot

- **Range:** $4,000+ / month
- **Fit:** multi-location group, high-ticket venue (> $25k average booking value), or 80+ inquiries / month
- **Setup intensity:** heavy — multi-venue setup, advanced reporting, custom integration scoping
- **Weekly review:** 60–90 min + on-demand check-ins
- **Setup fee:** $2,500–$5,000

## How to quote

In a discovery conversation:

1. **Diagnose** which tier they fit by asking about lead volume + average booking value + team size
2. **Frame the math** before quoting: "Your average booking is $X. If this pilot helps you recover even one extra wedding in 30 days, that's $X in influenced revenue."
3. **Quote the range, not a fixed number:** "For a venue like yours, pilots are typically in the [low|mid] four-figure range per month, plus a one-time setup fee."
4. **Close on the proposal, not the price:** "Want me to send a written proposal with the specific scope and number for your situation?"

If they push for a single specific number on the call, give the bottom of the range and confirm scope in writing later. Bottom-of-range stays honest; "starts at $X" gives you room to scope up.

---

## What's NOT included in the pilot

Be explicit about this in the proposal. Buyers value the honesty.

- ❌ Direct integration with The Knot or WeddingWire (no public API exists)
- ❌ Autonomous sending (operator approval required for every outbound reply)
- ❌ Instagram DM sending (until Meta App Review approves — currently in progress; operator-drafted in the interim)
- ❌ Guaranteed revenue increase
- ❌ SOC 2 / GDPR compliance certification (security questionnaire available on request)
- ❌ Multi-touch attribution or ROAS calculation (single-touch source attribution only)
- ❌ Replacement for HoneyBook / Tripleseat / your existing CRM (VenueRise sits alongside)

## Pilot → retainer conversion

End-of-pilot review meeting agenda:

1. **Walk the metrics** (response time, recovery, tour conversion, source visibility)
2. **Show the influenced revenue** (which bookings touched a Revenue OS surface during the pilot)
3. **Ask:** "Based on what we've measured, do you want to keep this running?"
4. If yes → quote month-to-month retainer (usually same tier as pilot, sometimes one tier up if they want deeper service)
5. If no → ask why. Close gracefully. Add to 90-day check-in queue in case circumstances change.

**Retainer pricing:** roughly the same range as pilot. Some buyers will negotiate down post-pilot; protect the floor (don't go below $750/mo even for starter — it's not worth your time).

## Edge cases

### "Can we do a free pilot?"

> Not at the moment. The pilot is paid because the value comes from us configuring it with you — that's services time. Free pilots tend to get less attention from both sides and underperform. The starter tier is structured so the cost is recoverable from a single booking influenced.

### "Can we do a longer pilot (60 / 90 days)?"

> Yes. Same monthly fee, same scope, longer measurement window. The 30-day version is the default because it's the smallest commitment that produces enough data to decide.

### "Can we skip the setup fee?"

> Sometimes — for high-intent, high-priority venues. Use sparingly. Waiving the setup fee on a low-intent buyer signals discount-seeking; waiving it on an A-tier venue you're excited about is a closing tool.

### "Can we do a per-booking / success fee?"

> Not in pilot. The attribution work isn't tight enough yet to make success fees fair — single-touch attribution overcounts on some channels and undercounts on others. After a few quarters of pilot data, this becomes possible. For now: flat monthly.
