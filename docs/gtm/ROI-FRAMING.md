# ROI Framing

How to talk about money with venue buyers — honestly.

The single most important rule:

> **VenueRise does not guarantee revenue. We frame value as *influenced pipeline* and *recovered opportunities,* not guaranteed bookings.**

That framing is more honest than competitor pitches AND closes better with sophisticated venue owners, because they've been pitched guarantees before and don't believe them.

---

## The simple formula

```
Recovered booking value = Average wedding booking value × additional bookings influenced
```

Example:

> If your average wedding booking is $15,000 and VenueRise helps you recover even 1 additional booking in a quarter, that's $15,000 in influenced revenue against a pilot cost in the low four figures per month.

This is the **one-extra-wedding framing.** It's the cleanest sentence in the sales toolkit because it requires zero claims and arithmetic the buyer can do in their head.

---

## The leakage formula

```
Pipeline at risk = sum of estimated budgets for leads in leakage buckets
```

Leakage buckets surfaced in the dashboard:

- Slow first reply (inquiries waiting > 1 hour)
- High-fit idle (qualified leads sitting without action)
- Qualified, no tour (couples ready for next step but not scheduled)
- Cold recovery (warm leads that went quiet)
- Reactivation (ghosted leads past the cooling window)
- Tour pending confirmation (scheduled but unconfirmed)

In a demo, point at the $124k "pipeline at risk" number on the demo seed and say:

> This is an estimate of how much pipeline is sitting in leakage buckets right now. It's not money you've lost; it's money that's at risk of going cold if nobody acts.

**Don't claim this number is precise.** It's estimated from operator-entered budgets per lead. The point is the *order of magnitude,* not the exact dollar.

---

## The tour conversion formula

```
Tour conversion lift = additional tours booked × close rate from tour × average booking value
```

Example:

> If VenueRise helps you book 5 extra tours in a month, and your tour-to-booking close rate is 30%, that's 1.5 expected bookings. At $15,000 average booking value, that's $22,500 in influenced pipeline.

This formula works because:
- The buyer almost always knows their tour close rate (or has a strong guess)
- The math is honest — you're multiplying through their own numbers
- It gives them a concrete sense of leverage without claiming anything you can't back

---

## Disclaimers (always include in writing)

When you put any of these numbers in a proposal or follow-up email, attach this:

> **Estimated, not guaranteed.** Influenced pipeline depends on the venue's lead volume, team process, follow-up consistency, and booking economics. The numbers above are illustrative — they're not commitments. VenueRise does not attribute single bookings to single touches; we report directional outcomes only. Ad spend is not connected to the platform unless specifically integrated, so this is not ROAS.

This sentence sits at the bottom of every proposal. The disclaimer makes the numbers more credible, not less.

## The one-extra-wedding framing (use early and often)

This is the most useful sentence in the whole pitch:

> For many wedding venues, one recovered booking can pay for months of the pilot. The pilot doesn't need to transform the entire business overnight. It just needs to prove that enough good leads are slipping to justify a system that catches them.

Use it:
- On the cold call when pricing comes up
- In the discovery conversation when you sense skepticism
- On the demo at minute 8:30 during the pilot offer beat
- In the follow-up email after the demo
- In the proposal under the pricing section

It works because:
- The math is true for almost every venue with average booking value > $5k
- It shifts the buyer's frame from "is this worth $X/month" to "is one extra wedding worth $X to me"
- It implicitly acknowledges risk ("doesn't need to transform the entire business")

## Talking about ad spend

VenueRise does NOT connect to ad platforms (Google Ads, Meta Ads). So we cannot calculate true ROAS.

What we can do:

- **Track per-source inquiry volume.** "You got 18 inquiries from The Knot this month."
- **Track per-source tour conversion.** "Of those 18, 4 tour-scheduled, 2 toured, 1 booked."
- **Track estimated per-source booked revenue.** "$15k in influenced booked revenue from The Knot this month."

What we cannot do:

- Pull spend from Google Ads / Meta / The Knot directly
- Calculate true ROAS (return on ad spend) per channel
- Multi-touch attribution

In conversations:

> We tell you which sources produced inquiries that became real bookings. We don't pull your ad spend from Google or Meta — that's not connected. So this isn't ROAS in the strict sense; it's source-to-booked attribution. But it's usually enough to redirect spend if you can see that The Knot is producing 3 bookings a month and your Instagram budget is producing zero.

## Pricing-against-value table

Use this internally to gut-check whether a pilot tier makes sense for a given venue:

| Average booking | Inquiries/month | Suggested tier | Months to break even on 1 extra booking |
|---|---|---|---|
| $5k | 20 | Starter ($1k–$1.5k) | 3–5 |
| $10k | 40 | Starter or Growth ($1k–$3k) | 3–10 |
| $15k | 60 | Growth ($2k–$3k) | 5–7 |
| $25k | 80+ | Growth or Premium ($3k–$4k+) | 6–8 |
| $50k+ | Multi-location | Premium ($4k+) | 10+ |

If the "months to break even on 1 extra booking" is > 12, the pilot doesn't make economic sense for that venue. Don't pitch it. Suggest they revisit when their inquiry volume or average booking value grows.

## What NOT to say about ROI

- ❌ "Guaranteed 3× more tours."
- ❌ "Our customers see X% more bookings on average." *(until you have written case-study evidence)*
- ❌ "ROI within the first 30 days." *(measurable outcomes, sure — but "ROI" is a commitment)*
- ❌ "Pays for itself within X bookings." *(close to the one-extra-wedding framing but more committal — soften to "many venues see one recovered booking cover months of cost")*
- ❌ "Recovers $X in pipeline per month." *(can't make per-month dollar promises)*

Honest substitutions:

- ✅ "Designed to help recover missed opportunities."
- ✅ "Influenced pipeline, not guaranteed revenue."
- ✅ "One recovered booking can cover months of pilot cost for most venues."
- ✅ "Surfaces leakage so your team can act before leads go cold."
