## The problem

Most wedding venues are not losing weddings because they have no leads.

They are losing them because good inquiries go unanswered, unqualified, unfollowed, or never pushed to a tour.

Inquiries arrive across website forms, Instagram DMs, The Knot, WeddingWire, Meta Ads, Google Ads, referrals, and email. The coordinator is in a tasting. The Tuesday inquiry doesn't get answered until Wednesday. By Wednesday the couple has booked at another venue.

The follow-up gap is where the money leaks.

---

## Why now

- Couples expect responses in hours, not days.
- Venue teams are small (1–3 people doing sales).
- Lead sources have multiplied (5+ channels is normal).
- Costs per lead from The Knot, WeddingWire, and Meta are rising.
- Generative AI can now draft on-brand replies that are good enough to send with one click of operator approval.

The combination of those five trends means the unit economics of a follow-up gap have gotten worse every year. Venues that don't close the gap give bookings to ones that do.

---

## The solution

**VenueRise is an AI Revenue OS for wedding venues.**

It connects your website, Instagram, The Knot, WeddingWire, Meta Ads, and inbox inquiries into one revenue dashboard, shows which leads are slipping, and drafts the next best reply for your team to approve.

---

## What VenueRise does

| Surface | What it shows / does |
|---|---|
| **Revenue Leakage Brief** | Estimated pipeline at risk + counts for slow replies, qualified-no-tour, recovery candidates, unconfirmed tours, source blind spots |
| **Leads board** | Stage-based view with leakage filters (slow first reply, high-fit idle, cold lead) so the team works the highest-value leads first |
| **AI drafts** | Pre-written replies for every new inquiry, follow-up, recovery, reactivation, and tour invite — operator approves before sending |
| **Inbox** | Unified across channels with source tagging on every message |
| **Tour confirmation queue** | Auto-surfaces scheduled-but-unconfirmed tours so they don't no-show |
| **Booked-revenue-by-source** | Tracks which inquiry sources are producing real booked weddings, not just leads |

---

## What VenueRise does NOT do

- Does not replace your CRM (HoneyBook, Tripleseat, Planning Pod). It sits alongside.
- Does not send messages autonomously. Every outbound reply requires operator approval.
- Does not promise guaranteed revenue.
- Does not claim SOC 2 / GDPR certification. Security questionnaire available on request.
- Does not have direct outbound API integration with The Knot or WeddingWire (no public APIs exist). Replies on those channels are operator-drafted, operator-sent.
- Does not calculate true ROAS (ad spend is not connected).
