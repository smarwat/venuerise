# Instagram DM Scripts

**Why DM:** wedding venue owners and sales coordinators live in Instagram more than email. A relevant DM often outperforms a cold email for this ICP. But the tone must match the channel — DMs that read like email get ignored.

**Rules:**
- Casual, but professional.
- Reference something specific you saw on their feed.
- One CTA per DM.
- Don't pitch the product in the first message.
- Never use generic compliments ("Beautiful venue!"). Everyone gets that.

---

## Short DM (initial outreach)

```
Hey [Venue Name] team — quick question.

Do you track which Instagram, website, and The Knot inquiries go
cold before they turn into tours?

I'm building VenueRise, an AI Revenue OS for wedding venues that
shows which leads are slipping and drafts the next follow-up for
your team to approve.

Would you be open to a quick 10-min walkthrough?
```

**Why this works:**
- Opens with a question, not a pitch
- Names the specific channels they actually use (including Instagram, which acknowledges where they live)
- Names the product + category in one line so they can search you up
- The ask is small (10 min)

---

## Story reply version

When they post a story (tour photo, behind-the-scenes, event setup), reply directly to the story:

```
Looks gorgeous — quick unrelated question. How does your team handle
the inquiries that come in from Instagram DMs vs the ones from your
website?
```

**Why this works:**
- The story reply context makes it feel one-to-one, not a blast
- "Quick unrelated question" signals you're not pitching the story content
- Asks about their workflow without naming the product yet

If they reply, then introduce VenueRise:

```
Got it — that's pretty common. We built a system that pulls all those
inquiries into one dashboard and shows which ones are slipping (no
reply, no tour booked, ghosted, etc.). Want me to send a 60-sec
explainer or just hop on a quick call?
```

---

## Follow-up DM 1 (3–5 days after initial)

If no reply to the initial DM:

```
Hey [Venue Name] — circling back on this.

Totally fine if it's not a priority. If it is, here's a 10-min
walkthrough link: [link]

Either way, no hard feelings.
```

---

## Follow-up DM 2 (7–10 days after follow-up 1)

The breakup version:

```
Last note from me — I'll stop reaching out if I don't hear back.

If revenue recovery from inquiries ever becomes a priority, I'm here.
```

---

## Voice note script (under 30 seconds)

Send a voice note when the venue is a high-priority target and text DMs aren't getting traction. Voice notes have a much higher reply rate in Instagram than text-only DMs.

```
Hey [Name], real quick — sending a voice note because I figured it
might be easier than another text wall.

I work with wedding venues on a specific problem — strong inquiries
going cold from slow follow-up across The Knot, Instagram, Meta
leads, and website.

If that's something your team has felt, would love to show you what
we've built in like 10 minutes. If not, totally cool to ignore this.

Either way, thanks for the work you do — your stories look great.
```

**Voice note tips:**
- Record in one take. Don't re-record three times. The imperfection is the point.
- Stand or walk while recording — voice sounds more natural.
- Don't read this script. Use it as the structure; say it like a human.

---

## What NOT to send via DM

| Bad | Why |
|---|---|
| "Hi! I have a software product for wedding venues!" | Reads as cold pitch, no curiosity |
| "Loved your last reel!!! 🔥🔥🔥" | Fake; everyone gets these |
| Multi-paragraph DM | DMs are mobile; long = skipped |
| Pricing in the first message | Premature; you haven't earned the ask |
| Asking to "hop on a quick call" with no context | "Quick call" without why = ignored |
| Adding them on every other channel after one DM | Stalker energy; kills the relationship |

---

## When to switch from DM to email

After 2 DMs with no reply, switch to email **only if** you have a verified business email for the venue (from their website footer, not LinkedIn scraping).

After 2 emails with no reply, stop. Move to a 90-day check-in.

---

## What "good" looks like

DM outreach to the right ICP:

| Metric | Realistic |
|---|---|
| Reply rate to initial DM | 8–15% |
| Voice note reply rate | 15–30% |
| Demos booked per 50 DMs | 1–3 |
| Demos → pilots | 1 in 4–6 |

DMs are higher-effort per send than email but have higher reply rates per send. Use them for your top-priority targets, not for cold blast.
